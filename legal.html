LEGAL, PRIVACY & COMPLIANCE

Governance & Engineering Policies

1. Privacy Policy

Adde Design Studio collects project brief information, land plot coordinates, and client portal verification data solely to deliver architectural design, engineering BOQ estimation, and construction management services. Form submissions are secured via Cloudflare Turnstile and encrypted within our private Google Workspace and ImageKit CDN environments. We never sell or share client data with unauthorized third parties.

2. Terms of Service & Intellectual Property

All conceptual 3D designs, BIM models, renders, and technical drawings showcased on this platform remain the intellectual property of Adde Design Studio. Upon full settlement of Design Phase contract milestones, clients receive a single-site construction license for the specified plot. Turnkey Construction works are governed by signed project-specific contracts referencing stamped Bills of Quantities (BOQ).

3. Engineering & Cost Estimator Disclaimer

Figures produced by our online East Africa Construction Cost Estimator and visual 3D concepts are preliminary planning tools derived from November 2026 regional averages. Final binding construction pricing and structural sign-off strictly require physical site surveying, geotechnical soil testing, and stamped architectural/structural drawings.
